# P3 targeted within-gene and composition sensitivity: prespecified protocol v1

Date: 16 October 2026. Target: approved scientific candidate 1.0.1-rc.1. This protocol is frozen before new sensitivity estimates are calculated or viewed. The historical manuscript estimates (+0.932547 and +0.651820), prior independent audit and earlier equal-gene sensitivities are already known. This is prospective specification of additional diagnostics, not outcome-blind preregistration or confirmatory inference.

## Fixed regions, labels and frozen reference

Only PLATEAU_90_120 = {90,105,120} and BROAD_60_120 = {60,75,90,105,120}; no new centers, region searches or tuning. Each center is an existing 31-nt window, not a continuous re-counted region.

Use original center-specific structural/mask eligibility: real unmasked length >=1 and every pseudo unmasked length >=1; every contributing transcript MULTI_PASS; consensus FIRST or INTERNAL; unique_gene_assignment YES and n_gene_ids=1. Canonical identity is chromosome, strand, genomic anchor, target-transcript set, gene, class and TMD-index set. FIRST and INTERNAL are annotations, not randomized exposures. Keep multiple FIRST anchors as well as multiple INTERNAL anchors and report their numbers. No arbitrary isoform or anchor selection.

Manifest: ER replicate 1 B01 PD/B02 IN, replicate 2 B11 PD/B12 IN; mitochondria replicate 1 B09 PD/B10 IN, replicate 2 B07 PD/B08 IN. Cytosol B03-B06 is verified but excluded from these region interactions. Replicate numbers are label matches, not independently verified culture/batch pairing.

For any class aggregate A, D(l,r,c,k)=log2(A_real,PD)-log2(A_real,IN)-log2(A_pseudo,PD)+log2(A_pseudo,IN). FI=D_FIRST-D_INTERNAL; I_r=mean_centers(FI_mito,r-FI_ER,r); displayed I=(I_1+I_2)/2. Library-wide scale factors cancel. Frozen reference uses equal-unit class densities before logs, available-case per-center populations, then equal centers and replicates. Independently reproduce both full-precision points and their saved 10,000-draw gene intervals. Acceptance: identical populations and absolute error <=1e-10; no widening after results.

## Populations and estimands

For region R define U_R as the union of genes in the frozen available-case rows. Define W_R as genes with at least one eligible FIRST and at least one eligible INTERNAL unit at EVERY center in R. All eligible units from those genes at each center remain; the same canonical units need not be available at every center. No averaging over a gene-specific subset of centers. Missing windows are missing, never zero-filled.

Let M(g,c,k,l,r,t) be the arithmetic mean density across all eligible units of gene g, class k, center c, library (l,r) and real/pseudo type t. INTERNAL multiplicity therefore does not give a gene more weight in M; multiple FIRST units are treated symmetrically.

Prespecified bridge comparisons, each separately for both regions:

1. **FROZEN_UNIT_ALL:** frozen equal-unit aggregate on U_R.
2. **EQUAL_GENE_ALL:** arithmetic mean of M over genes contributing to each class/center, followed by frozen log/contrast/region operations. Gene populations may differ by class/center. Retains zero densities.
3. **UNIT_SHARED:** frozen unit weighting restricted to W_R. Comparison with 1 isolates population restriction.
4. **EQUAL_GENE_SHARED:** equal gene/class/center density weighting on W_R. Comparison with 3 isolates weighting; shared membership balances all gene-level attributes across FIRST and INTERNAL. This is a ratio-of-density-means estimand, not a mean within-gene log contrast.
5. **UNIT_POSITIVE_SHARED** and **EQUAL_GENE_POSITIVE_SHARED:** restrict W_R to W+_R, where ALL 32 required M values per center (2 classes x 2 compartments x 2 fractions x 2 real/pseudo x 2 replicates) are strictly positive. The same W+ genes serve both replicates and every center. No epsilon, pseudocount, per-library gene deletion or positive-only center averaging. This restriction is outcome/measurement dependent; disclose its retention and covariate/coverage shift.
6. **WITHIN_GENE_MEAN:** for each g in W+ compute D_g separately by class/compartment/replicate/center, then J_g,r=mean_c[(D_g,F,mito,r-D_g,I,mito,r)-(D_g,F,ER,r-D_g,I,ER,r)]. Average J_g,r equally over genes, then the two replicates. Report gene-specific FI_ER,r and FI_mito,r as well as J_g,r. Compare with 5 to isolate aggregation-order change on EXACTLY the same population.
7. **WITHIN_GENE_MEDIAN:** median across genes of J_g=(J_g,1+J_g,2)/2. Replicate-specific medians are descriptive and need not average to the median of J_g. This robust-location estimand differs from 6; do not select between them using significance.
8. **WITHIN_GENE_COMMON_TRANSCRIPT:** repeat 6 on W+ genes for which the intersection of target-transcript IDs across ALL retained FIRST/INTERNAL units/centers is nonempty. No arbitrary representative transcript is chosen. Without this condition, describe 6 as a within-gene class-ensemble contrast, not a within-protein comparison. If fewer than 30 genes remain, report the point and conditional interval as sparse descriptive evidence, not a robustness pass.

A zero M excludes a gene only from explicitly positive-support estimands 5-8, never from the zero-inclusive alternatives 1-4. Report the exclusion reason by class, center and library. No compensation or imputation for excluded genes. If W+ is empty, mark these analyses not estimable and stop that branch.

## Composition balance and overlap

Construct one gene/class/center record with M and annotation summaries. Gene-level TMD count and CDS length must be identical for that gene across classes: median over distinct admissible target transcripts represented by the gene in the fixed five-center eligible inventory; also retain ranges and number of transcripts. CDS length is not total transcript length; do not substitute the latter label. Exact total transcript length is unavailable unless independently linked in existing verified metadata and is not required for balance.

For relative TMD position, use exact transcript/ordinal linkage to target TMD CDS start / target CDS length, average linked transcript positions within a canonical unit, then average units within gene/class/center. If a unit's set-valued metadata does not determine that linkage uniquely and no verified exact linkage table exists, mark position unavailable and exclude the affected gene/class record only from extended balancing; never manufacture the transcript-by-ordinal cross product. Ordinal index itself has structural non-overlap between FIRST and INTERNAL and will be diagnosed, not falsely balanced away.

Core coarsened exact strata: annotated TMD count [2,3-4,5-8,9+] x median CDS length [<1000,1000-1999,>=2000 nt]. For noninteger median count use boundaries [2,3),[3,5),[5,9),[9,infinity). Missing covariates are excluded from balance analyses and counted.

Extended strata add relative CDS position [0,.25),[.25,.5),[.5,.75),[.75,1]; input-only coverage category [zero, positive <= pooled positive median, positive > median] at each center; mean minimum(real,pseudo) unmasked fraction [<.5,>=.5]. Coverage is the mean of gene/class real and pseudo densities in B02/B10/B12/B08 input libraries. It is available before constructing PD/IN contrasts, but is part of their denominator, not independent pretreatment evidence. Extended balance is therefore a measurement/composition diagnostic, not causal adjustment.

At each center retain strata with at least FIVE distinct genes in EACH class. No merging, caliper tuning, regression extrapolation or fallback after results. Stratum target mass q_s is proportional to min(n_FIRST,s,n_INTERNAL,s). Give each gene/class in stratum s weight q_s/n_class,s; weights are shared across every library and real/pseudo type. Calculate balanced class density means before logs. Core and extended designs are frozen separately. At least 30 genes/class and Kish ESS>=30 in EVERY center and maximum class gene-weight share<=0.10 are required to call a balanced analysis supported; otherwise report lack of support without claiming robustness. No point estimate is extrapolated outside shared strata.

For each design also calculate **BALANCE_*_RESTRICTED_UNIT** and **BALANCE_*_RESTRICTED_EQUAL_GENE** on exactly the same retained gene/class/center records, before **BALANCE_*_WEIGHTED**. This separates population restriction, unit-to-gene weighting and further common-support standardization. These records can involve different genes by class; they are not within-gene comparisons.

Report weighted/unweighted continuous standardized mean differences (pooled within-class variance), distributions, retention, effective gene contributions and weights. Exact coarsened category balance does not guarantee continuous balance. |SMD|>0.10 is a residual-balance warning, not permission to tune. Include gene TMD counts, FIRST/INTERNAL contributions, ordinal/relative positions, CDS length, coverage, masking and missing-center distributions. Report annotation ambiguity explicitly.

## Uncertainty, influence and numerical criteria

Sampling unit is the GENE. Use 10,000 NumPy PCG64 multinomial whole-gene bootstrap draws, applying each gene's multiplicity jointly to all its classes/centers/libraries. The frozen reference uses original seeds 30097890 and 30097860, sorted gene IDs and batches 200 for direct interval verification. New variants use seeds 202610160000 + region_index*100 + variant_index, with region_index 1 plateau/2 broad and fixed variant order above, then core restricted-unit/equal-gene/weighted, then extended restricted-unit/equal-gene/weighted. Record actual seed mapping. Draws are conditional on observed genes, samples, annotation eligibility, W+ restriction and fitted balance design. Balance weights/strata are fixed within resamples: selection/fitting uncertainty is not covered. Genes do not create new biological replicates, nor ensure independence across shared genomic windows/pathways/libraries.

Report linear percentile 95% intervals and basic intervals [2*point-q97.5,2*point-q2.5] for EVERY valid alternative, without selecting a favorable method. Do not silently drop undefined bootstrap draws. Retain counts; if any invalid aggregates/draws occur, mark interval unavailable and investigate/report without changing estimator. No confirmatory P values or multiple-testing discoveries; all results shown. Gene n<30 is sparse descriptive evidence. Save bootstrap arrays and 99% Monte Carlo quantile-order bounds for percentile endpoints using binomial ranks; these bounds quantify simulation error, not biological coverage.

Delete each gene in turn and recompute the same estimator (balance design fixed, remaining weights renormalized). Record maximum absolute/relative change, gene, sign reversals, all gene deletion values, and per-replicate signs. For median retain the literal median definition. Sort ties by absolute change rounded to 12 decimals, then ascending gene ID; structurally zero effects rank together. Report TMD-count strata effects descriptively only if at least 30 eligible genes/stratum and every required aggregate is positive; no new confidence-based selection or additional regions.

Fixed material-change flags: sign reversal; any percentile/basic zero-crossing change versus frozen interval; |alternative-frozen|>=0.10 log2 OR relative magnitude change>=25%; interval-width change>=25%; maximum single-gene deletion change>=25% or any sign reversal; retention<50% of the relevant reference population; failed positivity/ESS or residual |SMD|>0.10. These diagnose sensitivity, not automatic scientific refutation. Same-population estimator comparisons are reported explicitly. A positive point is never evidence of a causal topology mechanism.

## Decision rules and audit trail

ROBUST WITHIN TESTED SCOPE requires supported comparisons retaining positive direction without material magnitude/influence flags; interval conclusions must be qualified if methods differ. PARTIALLY ROBUST / ESTIMAND-SENSITIVE applies when direction is retained but magnitudes/intervals/populations materially differ, or relevant restricted populations limit transfer. MATERIAL SCIENTIFIC CONCERN requires a verified numerical/data defect or a supported alternative materially undermining a stated broad claim (e.g. stable reverse direction on the relevant population), judged with estimand distinctions. INCONCLUSIVE applies if required verified inputs are absent or comparisons lack adequate support. Do not equate an alternative population effect with falsification of the frozen statistic.

Publish every prespecified branch/status, including infeasible/negative results. Preserve scripts, exact Python/package/platform information, input/output SHA256, logs, protocol hash/time, all population/weight records and calculations. Technical bug fixes may be made in the new workspace with a deviation log; no outcome-driven design changes. Independent implementation review and direct spot-checks precede the final interpretation. Manuscript edits, new releases and external changes are not authorized.
