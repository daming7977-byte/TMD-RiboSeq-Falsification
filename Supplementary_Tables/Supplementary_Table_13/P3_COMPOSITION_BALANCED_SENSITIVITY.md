# Composition-balanced sensitivity assessment

**Direction retained; magnitude and population are sensitive.** Core TMD-count/CDS-length standardization gives plateau 0.920510 and broad 0.683759 log2 units. Extended coarsened common-support standardization gives 0.590465 [0.119587,1.042098] and 0.408269 [0.034515,0.750890], reductions of 36.7% and37.4% from the frozen values. These exceed the prospective material-change thresholds. Basic intervals also remain positive: [0.138833,1.061344] and[0.065647,0.782022]. No preferred interval method was selected after results.

## What was standardized

Core strata: annotated TMD-count bins [2,3),[3,5),[5,9),[9,infinity), crossed with CDS-length bins <1000,1000-1999,>=2000 nt. Extended strata also include relative CDS-start quarters, input-only coverage zero/lowerpositive/upperpositive categories, and unmasked-fraction <0.5/>=0.5. Coverage is a stochastic denominator component of D, not independent pretreatment covariate evidence. Total transcript length including UTRs was not available and was not invented or replaced by a mislabeled CDS measurement.

Each retained stratum has at least 5 genes/class. Its target mass is proportional to min(n_FIRST,n_INTERNAL), allocated equally to genes within class. Weights are identical across samples and real/pseudo channels and fixed during resampling. No regression extrapolation, bin merging or post-result tuning occurred. Exact strata/weights are in `evidence/common_support_strata.tsv` and `evidence/balance_weights.tsv`; gene-level memberships are indexed in `P3_WITHIN_GENE_ELIGIBILITY_AND_BALANCE.tsv`.

## Support and residual imbalance

Core support retains 1749/1750 plateau and 1847/1848 broad genes in the union. Extended support retains 575/1750 (32.9%) and 966/1848 (52.3%). These are unions, not the number used at every center. At +120 only 78/472 FIRST genes (16.5%) and 106/1525 INTERNAL genes (7.0%) remain. At +90 the retained counts are 288/831 FIRST and 348/1697 INTERNAL. At +60/+75 broader-region counts are 481/1163 and 472/1158 FIRST, 574/1779 and566/1779 INTERNAL. No inference extends to discarded non-overlap populations.

All32 center/class overlap checks pass the prespecified minimum n/ESS/max-weight criteria. Minimum extended nominal Kish ESS is65.23 FIRST and77.35 INTERNAL; maximum individual nominal weight is1.887%. Exact coarsened stratum masses match between classes. This does NOT imply complete continuous balance. Extended relative-position SMD remains approximately-0.22 to-0.30; input-coverage SMD is+0.13 to+0.33, and some CDS-length, TMD-count and mask SMDs exceed0.10. These registered residual-balance warnings were retained; thresholds were not tuned to remove them. Ordinal index and retained TMD multiplicity are not balanced biological controls. See all means/SMDs in `evidence/balance_diagnostics.tsv` and empirical quantiles in `evidence/covariate_distributions.tsv`.

The within-shared-gene density alternative exactly balances gene-level TMD count and CDS length (SMD0), but positional and local coverage differences remain. This explains why gene matching and position/measurement standardization answer distinct questions. Core count/length balance alone does not remove the observed direction or appreciably attenuate it; the more restrictive extended design changes both the measured target population and weights.

## Gene contributions and influence

No leave-one-gene deletion reverses the overall interaction in any of the30 variants. However, the extended plateau maximum deletion change is 0.164540 (27.9%), for ENSG00000156709/AIFM1; broad is0.121273 (29.7%), for ENSG00000125991/ERGIC3. These exceed the prespecified 25% influence flag. They weaken a claim of uniform magnitude stability, even though direction persists. The original broad estimator retains its previously documented 36.7% maximum deletion change for ENSG00000251357; deleting it increases the estimate, so it is not a sole source of positivity.

Nominal equal-gene weights are not equal contributions to each density sum. In the existing full broad population, one required channel has a maximum gene density share 58.4% and effective density-contributor count 2.90; shared-gene density summaries have a maximum about 26.8% and minimum effective count 11.40. These concentration measures diagnose numerical influence, not effective biological sample size. Detailed channel records: `evidence/effective_density_contributions.tsv`. All single-gene effects are retained in `evidence/gene_deletion_influence.tsv`.

Prespecified TMD-count-stratum density estimates are all positive where n >= 30. Plateau shared-gene values by count bins are 0.750460, 1.375912, 1.043732, 1.476250; broad values 0.382092, 1.505708, 1.222716, 1.260421. They are descriptive heterogeneous strata, not a monotonic mechanism test. Within-gene log-stratum estimates were withheld because every stratum had fewer than 30 genes, as required by the protocol.

## Uncertainty scope

Every branch used 10,000 whole-gene draws, preserving classes/centers/libraries jointly. Zero invalid draws and zero invalid deletion estimates occurred. Both conditional mean-interaction percentile and basic intervals exclude zero in every branch, including the sparse branches; this does not override their support warning. Extended lower-endpoint 99% Monte Carlo bounds are [0.101987,0.132217] plateau and [0.018917,0.045705] broad, so the observed zero exclusion is not merely Monte Carlo sign jitter at these draw counts. These bounds do not cover biological or model uncertainty. Fitted balance selection, inherited preprocessing, region selection, cross-gene dependence, culture/batch pairing and biological confidence coverage remain outside this conditional exercise.

Replicate point concordance is not replicate-level interval concordance. Under extended standardization, replicate 1 percentile intervals cross zero in BOTH regions: plateau 0.462526 [-0.129430,1.064008], broad 0.344906 [-0.120817,0.799081]. Replicate 2 gives plateau 0.718404 [0.125523,1.220917] and broad 0.471631 [0.044475,0.841407]. These are still conditional gene intervals for fixed libraries, not biological replicate sampling intervals. The extended plateau mean interval is 119.3% wider than the frozen interval; broad is 2.6% wider. The complete results table retains all replicate intervals, basic intervals and width flags.
