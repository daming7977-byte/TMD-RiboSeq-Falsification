# Component rights and redistribution boundary

The 141 executable-source entries in `Code_for_Review/FILE_LEVEL_CODE_LICENSE_MANIFEST.tsv` cover all `.py`, `.sh`, `.mjs` and `.R` files present: 70 documented author-owned MIT entries, 70 inherited or vendored MIT entries, and one plastid-derived BSD-3-Clause entry. Existing copyright and permission texts are preserved. No original third-party notice or executable source is modified. Five author-owned historical scripts have path-prefix normalization only, recorded with original and current hashes.

Python/NumPy/pandas/matplotlib/Pillow and other runtime packages are dependencies to install separately, not redistributed binaries. No DeepTMHMM model/weights, full genome/GTF, raw FASTQ/BAM, confidential manuscript or acceptance letter is bundled. Wrapper code does not grant rights to external model services or databases.

Processed sequencing summaries, source data, annotation-derived geometry tables and scientific outputs retain their source provenance and applicable original terms. This package grants no new blanket data license and does not convert source attribution into ownership. The local reviewer materials document source accessions and provenance but do not supply a separate explicit reuse license for every source-data component. Final public redistribution terms must therefore be confirmed by the author/depositor where source-specific terms are absent; this review does not fabricate permission or assert that absence of a license prohibits redistribution.

The software part is ready for preservation of its existing MIT/BSD notices. The aggregate release remains conditional on source-rights confirmation, the chosen journal-compliant archival route and separate authorization. No public action has occurred.

Official source guidance inspected on 19 October 2026: [GEO disclaimer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html) states that NCBI places no data-use/distribution restriction but cannot grant unrestricted permission over submitter-held rights. [Ensembl disclaimer](https://www.ensembl.org/info/about/legal/disclaimer.html) permits use of project-generated data while preserving third-party constraints. The search service returned the official page text; direct page retrieval encountered a browser challenge/timeout. These general statements do not establish a new blanket license for all bundled derivatives. No specific conflicting third-party restriction was identified in the local records.
